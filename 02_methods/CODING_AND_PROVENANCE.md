# Coding and provenance

The fixed historical healthcare-specific stratum excludes S06 and S07 from 24 original coded records; S25 and S26 are contextual consumer-chatbot records. U01–U04 are contemporary illustrations, not additions to the historical denominator. The CSV flags and stable report IDs reproduce the published counts.

An outcome flag means the category was measured, not that benefit was proven. Referral intention is not completed referral; patient productivity is not provider workload. Clinical and organizational outcomes are separate dimensions. Historical appraisal labels are used only to reproduce the explicitly stated sensitivity scenario, not to claim a formal quality instrument.

The superseded 24-report baseline exists only to distinguish the effect of endpoint correction from eligibility changes. Source corrections remain documented in `05_provenance/data_corrections.csv`. The candidate queue is unassessed and cannot be read as a full-text exclusion list.

Source-check records preserve their documented access levels. S03 wording is reconciled with the retained random-assignment check; a new publisher full-text fetch was not successful in this packaging pass. S09 was rechecked against the primary article's Abstract, Methods—Participants and Results—Symptom Change on 23 October 2026. Enrollment was not back-calculated from completers.

The claim dispositions describe analyst-specified claims, not whole-study quality. They have not been independently rated or validated. Numerical checks validate the calculations, not completeness of source discovery or every source interpretation.
